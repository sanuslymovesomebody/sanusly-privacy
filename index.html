02 Data Collection, Storage & AI Moderation

We collect your email address, username, and posts. Data is stored via Google Firebase on EU servers (europe-west3, Frankfurt, Germany). Some metadata may be processed by Google in the USA, protected by EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

AI Moderation: To ensure a safe community, uploaded images and texts are automatically analyzed for inappropriate or prohibited content using Google Cloud Vision and Natural Language AI. Images are analyzed instantly and are not retained by the AI service beyond the duration of the check.

Legal basis: contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in maintaining a safe platform (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

03 Camera & Photo Library

We request camera and photo access solely to let you upload images to your posts. These images are processed for safety checks as stated in Section 2, stored securely in Firebase, and are not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

05 Push Notifications

With your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), we store your push token and timezone to send updates and motivational reminders. You can revoke this at any time in your device settings.

06 Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Upon account deletion, all personal data (email, posts, push token) will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our systems within 30 days.

07 Your GDPR Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR).
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate information (Art. 16 GDPR).
  • Erasure: Request full deletion of your account and data (Art. 17 GDPR).
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format (Art. 20 GDPR).
  • Withdrawal: Revoke consent at any time (Art. 7(3) GDPR).
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
